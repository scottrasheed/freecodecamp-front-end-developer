Community Garden Opens Downtown

Volunteers turn a vacant lot into a gathering place

By Scott Rasheed

Neighbors gathered Saturday to open a new community garden downtown. Volunteers spent the fall clearing the lot, building raised beds, and planting herbs along the walkway.

The garden will host free workshops next spring. Residents can sign up for a plot or help care for the shared beds on Saturday mornings.
