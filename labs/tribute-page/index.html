Dr. Norman Borlaug

The man who helped feed the world

Here is a timeline of Dr. Borlaug’s life:

  • 1914 – Born in Cresco, Iowa
  • 1944 – Began work in Mexico on wheat disease resistance
  • 1960s – Led the Green Revolution
  • 1970 – Received the Nobel Peace Prize
  • 2009 – Passed away at age 95
