# Cat Photo App

I built a simple cat photo gallery while working through freeCodeCamp’s 42-step Cat Photo App workshop. The page includes linked photos, lists of things cats love and hate, and captions for the images.

## Open locally

Open `index.html` in your browser. There’s nothing to install. The photos load from freeCodeCamp’s CDN and require an internet connection.
